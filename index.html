01 — ABOUT

Analyst first, researcher by training

I’m a GIS Analyst at the Global Geospatial Institute / Global Geospatial Solutions in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where I administer a production ArcGIS Enterprise deployment, build offline field systems for water utilities, and teach GIS to the next generation of analysts. My work sits at the intersection of enterprise GIS, UAV remote sensing, and machine learning.

I earned my M.S. from LSU with a specialization in GIS and remote sensing, where I spent three growing seasons flying multispectral drone surveys over rice research plots and benchmarking machine-learning models for yield prediction — work published as a first-author paper in Remote Sensing. That research foundation shapes how I build: measured, reproducible, and grounded in real field data.

03 — PROJECTS

Selected work

UAV · MACHINE LEARNING

Rice Yield Estimation from UAV Multispectral Imagery

Problem: predicting rice yield before harvest from drone imagery across multiple seasons and nitrogen treatments.

Role: designed the full pipeline — flight planning, Pix4D processing, vegetation-index extraction, and model benchmarking.

Outcome: 12-model benchmark reaching R² = 0.89; published as a first-author paper in Remote Sensing; reproducible Python workflow on GitHub.

View code Read the paper

EXPERIENCE BUILDER · SURVEY123

Gardere Community Hub

Problem: a Baton Rouge neighborhood needed one public place to find community resources and report local issues.

Role: built and deployed the Experience Builder app with an integrated Survey123 reporting survey.

Outcome: 4 operational layers and 60+ mapped community resources live; presented to project stakeholders in October 2026.

ARCGIS · GEOSTATISTICS

GIS-Based Soil Fertility Mapping — Ilam, Nepal

Problem: cardamom growers in Pandam, Ilam needed spatial guidance on soil fertility.

Role: interpolated soil pH, organic matter, N, P, K, and texture into continuous fertility surfaces using inverse-distance weighting in ArcMap with Geostatistical Analyst.

Outcome: published peer-reviewed article mapping fertility zones for the cardamom-growing area.
